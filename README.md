Entry for the make.fun bounty
[Sum up 22 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122)
($100, 50 places paid, ends 24 November 2026 00:00 UTC).

The entry is a newspaper front page that sums up the world's news of
Sunday 22 November 2026 in one image. `main.py` (Python 3.13) copies the
image held in this repository (`entry.png`, 1024x1024, under 1 MiB) to
`/output/entry.png`. It needs no network, and both runs write identical
bytes.

## The day's news in the image

- **COP31 talks collapse** — UN climate talks in Antalya, Turkiye, end
  without agreement after a dispute over fossil fuels.
- **Court rules Myanmar genocide** — the International Court of Justice
  rules Myanmar committed genocide against its Rohingya minority.
- **Tankers hit in Hormuz** — tankers attacked near the Strait of Hormuz;
  Bahrain reports an Iranian attack on an industrial facility.
- **Kyiv hit as winter nears** — Russian strikes hit an apartment
  building near Kyiv, with people missing and killed.
- Foot strip: US questions Nvidia's AI-chip sales to China; Nigeria boat
  tragedy; Indonesia factory fire.
